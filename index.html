About Make In BVB

Make In BVB (MIB) is the student body of KLE CTIE at KLE Technological University, Hubballi. Since 2014 it has run workshops, competitions and flagship events like PUPA, E-Summit and Butterfly to help students turn ideas into products.

Software cluster

Web, app and AI products, plus the platforms MIB runs on all year.

Design cluster

Brand, UI and UX for MIB projects, and design sprints for everyone.

Hardware and IoT

Circuits, sensors and prototypes built in the lab and demoed live.

Operations

Logistics, sponsors and outreach that keep every event running.
